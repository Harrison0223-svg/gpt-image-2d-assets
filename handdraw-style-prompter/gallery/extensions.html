Game Art Styles · G01–G24

Browse the 261 reference styles

Pick a recipe or combine a primary style with a few specific traits. These are text recipes, not validated production masters.

G01 · Pixel Cluster

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Limited color ramps, clear pixel clusters, hard silhouettes and restrained dithering.

Useful for: characters, icons, tiles

Set the target pixel size first. Check stray pixels and non-integer sampling.

G02 · Arcade Pixel

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Compact shading ramps, metallic highlight blocks, strong warm/cool contrast and sharp silhouettes.

Useful for: characters, enemies, explosions

Keep complex texture from obscuring action silhouettes.

G03 · Clean Cel

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Clean variable-width outlines, two or three hard shading levels, broad color areas and sparse highlights.

Useful for: characters, props, animation

Keep shadow shapes and light direction consistent between frames.

G04 · Gouache Storybook

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Opaque gouache blocks, soft grain, rounded forms and a limited warm palette.

Useful for: characters, scenes, cards

Do not carry a paper backdrop into transparent assets. Keep silhouettes readable when reduced.

G05 · Watercolor Ink

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Fine ink lines, translucent washes, local blooms and sparse pigment edges.

Useful for: portraits, props, illustration

Control subject opacity; paper white is not transparency.

G06 · Dry Brush Ink

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Bold ink masses, dry-brush gaps, pale ink layers and sparse vermilion accents.

Useful for: characters, spells, scenes

Inspect broken brush edges and smoke on light and dark backgrounds.

G07 · Mineral Fine Line

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Fine controlled outlines, flat mineral colors, restrained layering and orderly ornament.

Useful for: character art, props, UI

Reserve fine patterns for large images and simplify them at UI scale.

G08 · Woodcut

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Strong black-and-white masses, carved hatching, few spot colors and angular edges.

Useful for: monsters, cards, icons

Avoid line densities that create moire at the target size.

G09 · Etching

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Fine crosshatching, delicate edges, local white space and monochrome foundations.

Useful for: equipment, items, illustration

Reduce hatching density in small images while retaining silhouette.

G10 · Cut Paper

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Scissor-cut edges, flat color pieces, layered silhouettes and slight handmade irregularity.

Useful for: characters, icons, UI

Do not turn shadows between layers into a solid backdrop.

G11 · Risograph

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Two- or three-color overprinting, controlled misregistration, grainy ink and flat shapes.

Useful for: cards, posters, backgrounds

Misregistration must not disrupt UI positioning or animation anchors.

G12 · Fabric Applique

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Fabric-piece silhouettes, stitched borders, soft fibers and warm palettes.

Useful for: items, characters, UI

Keep shapes stable and prevent stitches from becoming random noise.

G13 · Clay Sprite

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Rounded volumes, matte clay, restrained impressions and soft fixed lighting.

Useful for: characters, props, icons

The output remains a 2D raster image, not editable 3D.

G14 · Enamel Toy

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Rounded contours, smooth enamel, concentrated highlights and clear material boundaries.

Useful for: icons, items, equipment

Maintain highlight direction without blowing out the silhouette.

G15 · Flat Vector Look

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Precise geometry, clean flat fills, few layers and consistent corner radii.

Useful for: icons, UI, markers

The output is raster art, not SVG vector paths.

G16 · Decorative Line

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Continuous ornamental lines, repeated motifs, varied line weight and limited gold accents.

Useful for: UI, borders, equipment

Keep decoration from impairing text contrast or hit-area readability.

G17 · Rubber Hose

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Elastic curved limbs, simple black-and-white masses, rounded silhouettes and exaggerated faces.

Useful for: characters, animation, expressions

Distinguish intentional elastic joints from identity or limb-count drift.

G18 · Halftone Comic

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Strong silhouettes, flat shadows, controlled halftones and motion lines.

Useful for: characters, effects, cards

Add lettering only when requested. Keep action lines out of neighboring cells.

G19 · Colored Pencil

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Visible pencil hatching, light color layering, soft edges and varied pressure.

Useful for: portraits, props, illustration

Do not attach a paper rectangle to transparent subjects. Check thin-line loss when scaled.

G20 · Soft Pastel

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Soft pastel layers, locally powdery edges, broad gentle volumes and a few sharp focal edges.

Useful for: backgrounds, portraits, spells

Avoid uniform blur; interactive objects need distinct edges.

G21 · Gothic Painterly

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Elongated shapes, cool dark foundations, concentrated warm light and restrained weathering.

Useful for: monsters, scenes, cards

Retain value separation and readable silhouettes in dark areas.

G22 · Isometric Painted

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Consistent isometric projection, distinct top and side planes, painted color blocks and fixed lighting.

Useful for: buildings, props, tiles

Keep camera and footprint sizes consistent. Check seams when tiling is required.

G23 · Neon Graphic

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Dark solid structures, saturated rim light, narrow glows and geometric rhythm.

Useful for: effects, icons, UI

A dark preview does not prove alpha. Preserve glow gradients.

G24 · Folk Flat

Text recipe; review a generated sample before production.

Naive shapes, a vivid limited palette, ordered botanical motifs and handmade edges.

Useful for: characters, cards, scenes

Keep patterns subordinate to the subject. Do not add unrequested plants.
